KUBERNETES HEALTH PROBES

Probes are checks performed by the kubelet to determine whether a container has started, can receive traffic, or needs restarting.
1. STARTUP PROBE
Question:
Has the application finished starting?

Useful for applications that take time to initialize, such as our Java application.

On failure:
Kubernetes retries. After failureThreshold consecutive failures, the container is terminated and restarted according to its restart policy.

Until startup succeeds, liveness and readiness checks do not run.
2. READINESS PROBE
Question:
Can this application instance receive traffic?

On failure:
The pod becomes NotReady and is excluded from normal Service traffic. The container continues running.

When the probe succeeds again, the pod can receive traffic again.

Example:
The application is temporarily unable to serve requests.
3. LIVENESS PROBE
Question:
Is the application functioning, or is it stuck and needs restarting?

On failure:
After failureThreshold consecutive failures, the container is terminated and restarted according to its restart policy.

Example:
The application process is running but is deadlocked.

Avoid making liveness depend on a shared database being available. A database outage could otherwise trigger unnecessary restarts across all pods.

IMPORTANT SETTINGS

initialDelaySeconds:
Wait before starting checks.

periodSeconds:
How often to check.

timeoutSeconds:
How long to wait for a response.

failureThreshold:
Consecutive failures before taking the probe’s failure action.

OUR APPLICATION

Our handover lists these Spring Boot endpoints:

Readiness:
/actuator/health/readiness

Liveness:
/actuator/health/liveness

Endpoint availability alone does not confirm that probes are configured in the Deployment. Startup-probe configuration also needs verification.

ALB target health checks are separate from Kubernetes probes.

INTERVIEW ANSWER

“Startup probes allow time for initialization before readiness and liveness checks begin. Readiness failures stop normal traffic to the pod without restarting it. Liveness failures trigger a container restart after the configured threshold.”
